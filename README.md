**Common Sense** gives mobs some common sense, for Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge.

## Companions

- **Pets, golems and anything on a lead** keep clear of lava and cliffs and keep up with you.
- They **walk up hills** instead of being yanked, and come down the safe way.
- In boats or on ice they get **dragged along** instead of snapping the lead.
- Pets are **rescued** from drowning, lava and deadly falls, and only teleport onto safe ground.

## Villagers and golems

- Villagers **remember their beds** and find their way home when lost.
- They **follow a player holding an emerald block**, and stay put when you move them somewhere.
- Golems and guards **walk back to their village**.

## Herd instinct

- Hurt one cow and **the rest of the herd runs**.
- Hit a spider and **its friends come for you**.

## And

- Sleeping mobs stay visible at normal distances.
- Every feature has its own toggle in **Mods → Common Sense → Config**.

## Bugs and ideas

Please post them in **[Issues](https://github.com/SteelArchadian/CommonSense/issues)**. That's where every report gets read and tracked, rather than in CurseForge or Modrinth comments.

## Building

```
./gradlew build
```

The jar is written to `build/libs/`.
